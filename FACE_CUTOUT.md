# Face Cutout Ultra Precision

Experience lab-grade face and hair segmentation for adults, kids, and pets with strand-level detail and zero background bleed. Every pixel of the subject remains bitwise faithful while the background is fully transparent (alpha=0) for flawless compositing.

## Why it is different
- **Pixel fidelity**: Bitwise-stable subject pixels—no softening, smearing, or color drift.
- **Transparent by default**: True alpha background for instant drop-ins to any design stack.
- **Hair-level edges**: Captures curls, flyaways, fur, and whiskers with micro-detail accuracy.
- **Universal subjects**: Optimized for adults, children, and pets across varied lighting and poses.
- **Print-ready output**: High-resolution files suitable for apparel, signage, stickers, and merch.

## How it works
1. **Upload an image** (or drop a file, paste from clipboard, or provide a URL).
2. The engine performs **semantic cropping** with **mask integrity checks** to keep the subject untouched and the background voided.
3. Export with **transparent PNG** or embed directly into your canvas pipeline.

## Quality safeguards
- Multi-pass edge validation to preserve eyelashes, glasses, and fine jewelry.
- Geometry-consistent masks to avoid warping faces or altering proportions.
- Zero hallucination: No invented pixels—only the original subject is retained.
